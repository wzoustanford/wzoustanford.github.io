Will Y. Zou

    Welcome to my homepage. I work in on ML research and AI products at scale. The culture of building effective and scalable systems enables AI creation and assistance. The use and application of AI should be safe and trusting. Researchers may also see me at conferences and curiously working on AI papers. Right now, I'm open to working with engineers, investors on new innovations.

    With interest in deep learning and scalable production ML systems, my resarch highlights include action recognition with Independent Subspace Analysis, and object detection with convolutional networks. With natural languages, we took the word-embedding idea to machine translation. I also worked simulation of cognition of approximate visual number sense and heterogeneous causal learning for contextual bandits.

    Obtained B.A. and M.Eng. from University of Cambridge, Ph.D. at Stanford University. During PhD, I had the fortune to work with the Stanford AI Lab, NLP Group, Stanford Psychology, and NEC Labs America Media Analytics Group on deep learning applications to computer vision. In industry, I have worked with many wonderful people and teams over the years.
